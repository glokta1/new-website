---
title: "The joy of \"locking in\""
pubDate: 2024-10-23
---

What does it feel like to "lock in"? I've "locked in" in various contexts (studying, football, chess)

* Feels like you can see everything coming before it happens.
* The difficulty level is just right so it's not frustratingly difficult but not piss easy either
* A buzz in the brain, an ache? Similar to a sore muscle after a heavy gym session.
* I can make connections between concepts 5x faster.
